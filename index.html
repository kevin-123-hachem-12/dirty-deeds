    How to play

    The rules of mischief

    • Click or tap to creep somewhere. Click objects to grab items, set traps or hide.
    • On a phone: drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap House to see the whole building. The camera follows you again when you move.
    • He follows a routine. His thought bubble says where he's going next.
    • Same room and not hidden means you're caught. You have three hearts: each catch costs one, and losing all three ends the day. The yard is always safe.
    • Climb the drainpipe from the yard to the kitchen window. He can only use the stairs.
    • Glue holds him in place. Water soaks floors for 20 seconds. Electric traps are strong but noisy: if he's nearby he hears you and comes to look.
    • Combos score double: electricity on a wet floor, or anything while something is glued to him.
    • Every prank raises his temper. At Furious he walks faster and checks hiding spots.
    • Do every deed to finish the day. Finish with 30 seconds or more on the clock for 3 stars, before it hits zero for 2, or in overtime for 1.
    • New pranks: animals send him running out of the room, food sends him hunting for water, and noise pranks go off a few seconds after you set them and lure him over.
    • He gets smarter: he checks spots where he was pranked, notices missing things, searches the hiding spot he last found you in, and on harder days sets traps. Tap a trap to disarm it.
    • From the garage on, Gerald's bulldog Biscuit sometimes patrols downstairs. If he sees you he barks and Gerald comes running. Feed him the sausage from your yard to keep him busy for 15 seconds, but while you carry it he follows you and whines.
    • Tap once to sneak, silently. Double-tap to run: much faster, but he hears your footsteps if he's on the same floor.
    • Tap an item in your pockets and the spots that need it light up. You start every day with a bag of marbles: tap it, then tap somewhere nearby to throw one. Gerald goes to look at the noise. Three per day.
    • A red glow around the screen means he's coming into your room. Hide!
    • Every prank snaps a photo for your Photo Album. Stars unlock Rewards: gadgets that add a bonus prank to every day, and hats for the Prankster.
    • Waiting for him? Press and hold the fast-forward button in the corner (or hold F) to run time three times as fast. The gear button pauses the day, with sound, music, restart and the main menu inside. P also pauses.
